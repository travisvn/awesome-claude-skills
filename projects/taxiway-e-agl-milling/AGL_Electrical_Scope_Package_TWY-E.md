No cutting shall proceed over an unresolved discrepancy.
3. **Electrical isolation.** The affected series circuit(s) shall be isolated, locked out, tagged and proven dead at the CCR under an approved permit-to-work before any intrusive activity. Re-energisation shall occur only after formal permit cancellation and satisfactory IR testing.
4. **Protection of cables during milling.** Milling shall not commence over any area containing AGL assets until all secondary cables have been exposed, sleeved and secured clear of the milling depth, and all duct crossings with insufficient residual cover have been protected by the approved method. A dedicated AGL banksman shall accompany the milling machine at all times within the demarcation.
5. **Exposing secondary cables.** Secondary cables shall be exposed by hand excavation only; no mechanical excavation is permitted within [500 mm] of any as-built cable or duct alignment. Exposed cables shall be visually inspected, and any damage reported immediately to the Engineer before concealment.
6. **Coring methodology.** Coring at light fitting positions shall be concentric with the existing fitting/base per the As-Built, to the approved diameter and depth, with continuous water management to prevent slurry ingress into bases, ducts and transformer housings.
7. **Reinstatement.** All cores, saw cuts and trenches shall be reinstated to the approved specification (bedding, backfill, compaction, sealing). Fittings shall be re-levelled to the new finished pavement surface within the tolerance of [±1.5 mm], torqued to specification, and orientation-checked before final seating.
8. **Coordination with Civil.** The AGL team shall attend the Civil pre-start briefing daily; milling sequence, depths and machine routing within the demarcation shall be agreed jointly each shift. Any change to the milling extent or depth invalidates this drawing until re-verified against the As-Built.
9. **Airside safety.** All works shall comply with the airport's airside safety procedures, work permit conditions, ATC coordination requirements, low-visibility restrictions, FOD prevention and vehicle escort rules. The work area shall be inspected FOD-free before reopening of the taxiway.
10. **Records.** All exposed assets, test results, discrepancies and reinstatement details shall be photographed, logged and incorporated into the red-line mark-up for as-built updating on completion.

# SECTION C — GENERAL METHODOLOGY NOTE

## Taxiway E (Between E & E6) — Electrical / AGL Works Associated with Civil Milling — Basis of Set-Out and Measurement

**1. The As-Built drawing is the base layer.**
The approved As-Built drawings for Taxiway E constitute the single source of truth for the position, alignment, depth and identity of all existing AGL and electrical assets, including primary series circuits, secondary cables, isolating transformers, light fittings, 12-inch and 8-inch main duct crossings, cable pits and manholes. Every drawing in this package is constructed with the As-Built as the base layer, and every dimension, chainage, offset and asset position stated herein is taken from, and traceable to, the referenced As-Built drawings.

**2. The Civil demarcation is an overlay only.**
The Civil milling / asphalt drawings serve one purpose in this package: to define the demarcation limits of the three milling locations. These demarcation boundaries are overlaid onto the As-Built base to identify which existing assets fall within, or within the influence zone of, each milling area. The Civil drawings shall not be used to derive the position of any existing asset, and no asset position shall be scaled, inferred or transposed from the Civil drawings.

**3. All cutting, coring and cable works are measured only from as-built positions.**
Every saw cut, core, hand excavation and cable exposure shall be set out on site exclusively from the as-built coordinates, chainages and offsets, confirmed by joint site verification (cable locator / EMF sweep) before the first intrusive activity at each location. Quantities in the Affected Assets Tables and the Material Requirement Sheet are, and shall remain, derived from the as-built record and the verified overlay — never from assumption, interpolation between sources, or field estimation.

**4. Coordinate discipline — previous-phase issues shall not be repeated.**
Coordinate discrepancies experienced in previous phases arose where positions were taken from mixed references or unverified sources. To prevent recurrence, the following controls are mandatory for this package: (a) a single coordinate system and datum, as stated on the As-Built, shall be used on all drawings and set-out; (b) the Civil demarcation shall be transformed onto the as-built grid and the overlay jointly reviewed and signed off by the Electrical and Civil leads before issue of each shop drawing for construction; (c) any discrepancy found between site conditions and the As-Built shall stop work in the affected zone and be formally reported, resolved and documented before works resume; and (d) all deviations shall be captured on red-line mark-ups and incorporated into the as-built update at completion, so that the record remains the reliable base for future phases.

**5. Hold points.**
The following are mandatory hold points requiring the Engineer's release: (i) completion of the joint overlay verification at each location; (ii) confirmation of circuit isolation prior to first cut/core; (iii) inspection of exposed cables and protected ducts prior to milling over them; (iv) inspection of bedding and cable reinstatement prior to backfilling; and (v) acceptance testing prior to re-energisation and reopening of the taxiway.
