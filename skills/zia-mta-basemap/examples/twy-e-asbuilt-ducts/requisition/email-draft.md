Please find attached the final scope shop drawings for the AGL rectification works on Taxiway E between E4 and E6, together with the installation programme and a material requisition for your approval.

**1. Scope — Rev P08 is the final scope of work**

Drawing AUH-SK-AGL-TWYE-001 Rev P08 (sheets 1001 / 1002 / 1003) supersedes the Rev P06 / P07 field-sheet quantities. The scope is now:

- **19 No. affected fittings** across the three milling locations — 14 No. at Location 1, 1 No. at Location 2, 4 No. at Location 3.
- **11 No. existing bases cored out** (6 @ 8" and 5 @ 12"), and **13 No. new side-entry shallow bases** installed (11 @ 8" and 2 @ 12").
- **6 No. fittings take new secondary cable only** with no coring — 3 No. at Location 1 (SBC102-02/027, TCCECH-03/035, TCCECH-03/018), which keep their existing base under a dummy plate during milling, and 3 No. at Location 3 whose existing 12" base accepts the new side entry.
- **Approximately 420 m of saw cut** and **19 No. secondary cable runs**, one 2-core 4 sq.mm cable to each fitting, laid manhole to light with no joints.

**2. Secondary duct — position agreed and closed**

The existing 4 x 19 mm secondary duct bank was exposed at 50 mm milling depth. As agreed between the AGL team, the civil team and ADA AGL:

- The existing 4 x 19 mm duct cannot accommodate the new secondary cable, and the existing cables are obsolete and are to be withdrawn.
- The civil scope of work covers the milling area only, so a full-stretch duct cannot be delivered under this work.
- **Saw cut is therefore adopted for every milling-affected asset as an interim arrangement**, with permanent duct provision to follow under the South Rehabilitation works, where the saw cut routes will be replaced with duct to the standard design.

Technical queries Q1, Q2 and Q3 are closed on that basis.

**3. Programme — five working shifts, one location at a time**

The installation is planned over five shifts, each location cut, cabled, tested and sealed before the next is opened:

| Day | Location | Work |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Location 1 (1001) | Setting-out, ~300 m saw cut, 11 No. 8" bases, 14 No. cable runs, test and reinstatement |
| 4 | Location 3 (1003) | ~95 m saw cut, 1 No. 12" base, 4 No. cable runs, test, reinstatement, RRM re-fix |
| 5 | Location 2 (1002) | ~24 m saw cut, 1 No. 12" base, 1 No. cable run, then circuit commissioning and handover |

Testing and commissioning is carried out at circuit level and covers the affected fittings together with the other fittings on the same circuits, so the series circuits are proved end to end before handover.

**4. Two hold points gate the start**

The five days run consecutively from the release of both of the following, and **neither is released at the date of this revision**:

- **H1 — Curing complete.** Written confirmation from the civil team that the asphalt curing period is complete.
- **H5 — Saw cut detail.** The saw cut and side-entry shallow base detail drawing issued and accepted. No saw cutting can start before it, and it also fixes the cut width and depth, the core diameters and the sealant specification, on which our material quantities depend.

**We would be grateful for the early issue of the saw cut detail drawing**, as it sits on the critical path for the whole five-day programme.

**5. Signboard masking**

The direction signboards leading to E4 and E6 will be masked with matt black vinyl sticker under Phase 1 before works commence, and unmasked in Phase 3 before the functionality check and return to operational service.

**6. AGL assets within the closed area**

AGL assets inside the closed area pass into the civil team's care for the duration of the closure. Any damage to an AGL asset inside the closed area is to be made good under the civil scope, and is to be reported to the AGL Team Leader on discovery so that it is recorded and rectified before handover.
